## Contract statements he forwarded

On the gap:
> The intended repository retrieves knowledge and resolves a deterministic score, but its current
> strategy executor does not steer an LLM through the scene decisions the owner described.
> Copying the other compiler wholesale would create a competing semantic authority.

On the handoff into the score:
> The next slice should carry this session's characters, ordered beats, actions, contacts, camera
> choices and 15-second duration into the existing score, preserving origins, locks and
> relationships. Merely making the lists nonempty is insufficient. The target's current Veo
> profile permits 4, 6 or 8 seconds. A correct bridge must preserve the requested 15 seconds and
> report that incompatibility, rather than silently shorten the fight.

On layers and sublayers:
> Reasoning inside the Laban layers and camera sublayers, as well as between the larger creative
> passes. The scratchpad should hold one shared scene state, with decisions attached to their
> layer or sublayer. Each decision records its inputs, chosen values, relative anchor, brief
> justification and source status. Python supplies the relevant context and validates proposed
> changes. … Those camera choices must work together as a stack. A framing choice that hides the
> movement's essential initiation needs revision or an explicit creative justification. … Giving
> every sublayer an isolated prompt would lose those relationships. Requiring identical LLM
> choices would also confuse creative judgment with determinism. The LLM chooses the creative
> relationships; Python checks their consistency and compiles the accepted decisions reproducibly.

On research:
> Research supplies the knowledge and taste guidance for every relevant layer and sublayer. The
> LLM applies it to the user's scene. Each scratchpad decision should link to the research it
> used, explain briefly how it applies, and distinguish sourced guidance from authored choices. …
> Research-backed reasoning does not make every creative choice a research finding. Preserve the
> distinction between sourced knowledge, creative application and model-tested results.

On two findings that must not become hard rules:
> Phase-specific wording is the useful requirement [not a claimed contradiction]. Structured JSON
> and relative prompting are compatible. Preserve numeric scene controls and anchor relationships;
> choose their provider representation separately. `r005` supports retaining the structured fight
> route, but does not establish universal superiority.
