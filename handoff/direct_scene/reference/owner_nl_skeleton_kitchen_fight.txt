GOAL      15s · 16:9 · A close-quarters fight in a narrow restaurant kitchen.
          Starts: DAI attacking, LIN backing away. Ends: DAI's last punch stopped in both of
          LIN's hands, the two of them locked.
MOTION PRIORITY   For every beat, in this order: what each fighter is trying to do > the distance
          between them > stance > set-up > contact > reaction > recovery > camera readability.
STYLE     Modern high-budget 2D anime action. Multi-limb smears on fast strikes, sharp poses
          between them, one stylised impact flash. May not change: the order of events, who
          touches what, who is who.
LOOK      Dark, desaturated: steel grey, white tile, black, with LIN's yellow jacket the only
          strong colour. Hard white light from one strip lamp over the counter on the left, deep
          shadows on the tiled side, slight bloom on the steel.
PACE      Full fighting speed, no slow motion, no pauses between strikes. Every block, duck and
          stumble happens at the instant of the hit, never a beat later. One pause, one second
          long, at the very end.

CAST      DAI:  tall, shaved head, black chef's jacket with rolled sleeves, tattooed forearms.
                Effort: strong, sudden, direct, bound: every strike is held tight and can stop
                dead. Body: drives from the hips, weight forward.
                Shape: tall and broad, advancing. Face: eyes fixed on his target, never blinking.
          LIN:  short black bob, yellow rain jacket, blue jeans, red sneakers.
                Effort: light, quick, evasive, free: she lets movement run on and carry her.
                Body: moves from the feet, weight on the back leg.
                Shape: small, crouched and turned side-on, retreating. Face: wide eyes, checking
                for exits.
          Exactly two people.

WORLD     A narrow restaurant kitchen after closing. Left side: a long steel counter with pans
          hanging above it under the strip lamp. Right side: a white tiled wall with one broom in
          a wall clip, and further along it a tall wooden shelving unit. Far end: a steel fridge
          door. Wet tile floor. The counter is always on the same side. DAI starts nearer the
          camera, LIN further in. Whenever both are seen from the side, DAI is on the left of
          frame and LIN on the right. There is only one broom.
PHYSICS   The fridge door is solid steel and takes a body hitting it with a boom. The broom is a
          long wooden handle with a bristle head; it pulls out of its wall clip with one hard
          yank, and the handle snaps under a sharp downward blow when it is held at both ends. The
          shelving is wood: a handle tip can lodge in its side panel. Sneakers slide easily on
          the wet tile. Neither fighter can lift the other. The broom handle reaches further than
          DAI's kick. DAI is the heavier hitter: his kick moves LIN's whole body, while her blocks
          only turn his strikes aside. A body that is moving keeps moving until a wall or the
          floor stops it. The broom needs both hands, and each thrust carries LIN half a step
          forward with it.
ANCHORS   Normal speed and force = DAI's three strikes in beat 1. Normal reach = DAI's arm.
RULES     Contact: forearms, palms and crossed arms only; never clean on a face or body.
          Reaction: the body that is hit moves at the speed of the hit, at the same instant.
          Counts: three opening strikes, three broom thrusts, four strikes in beat 9, one flash.
          Range: every beat states the distance between them. It changes only through a step,
          a lunge or a blow that is shown.
          Inserts show a detail of the action in progress; they are never a new event.
          The fight continues during an insert; the next beat opens on the result.
PHRASES   1 Pressure (beats 1-3) · 2 Reach (beats 4-6) · 3 Loss (beats 7-8) · 4 Close (beats 9-10)

BEAT 1 (2.5s) ANCHOR A
  TACTIC   DAI: overwhelm her before she can set her feet. LIN: survive and get distance.
  RANGE    an arm's length apart; LIN gives half a step with each strike and DAI follows
  CAM      medium two-shot from beside the counter, slightly tilted, drifting handheld; both
           fighters seen from the side, DAI on the left, LIN on the right. The camera gives
           ground with LIN because she is the one being driven back; it starts with both in a
           medium frame and ends slightly closer.
  DO       DAI throws three fast hand strikes, arms smearing
  WHY      his strikes come straight and fast because he wants her overwhelmed before she can
           set; she gives ground because every block costs her her balance
  ANSWER   LIN turns two aside with her forearms and ducks the third
  READ     each fist meeting a forearm must be visible between the two bodies
  REACT    her head and shoulders jolt with every hit; her feet scramble back as fast as his hands

BEAT 3 (2s) ANCHOR A, wider
  TACTIC   DAI: finish it with one heavy blow. LIN: absorb it and stay on her feet.
  RANGE    DAI hops half a step back to make room; they are a full leg-length apart
  CAM      the camera slides back with LIN because she is thrown; it moves smoothly at her speed,
           starts on both bodies head to foot and ends on her against the fridge door with DAI
           still in frame, far from the fridge
  DO       DAI throws one straight front kick, much harder than his hand strikes, his leg fully
           extended at impact and both his hands up by his own chest
  BODY     left foot plants, hips drive forward, right leg shoots out fully straight
  EFFORT   DAI at his strongest and most direct so far; LIN gives way, light and loose
  SHAPE    one long line from his planted foot to the sole of his boot
  SPACE    the kick travels straight forward at chest height; LIN travels straight back, low
  WHY      he commits his whole weight through the hips because he wants one blow to end it;

  CONTACT  only the sole of his boot, onto LIN's crossed forearms
  READ     the boot landing on her forearms with his leg straight, then her feet leaving their
           place
  REACT    forearms fold into her chest; she is thrown backward down the kitchen, away from him,
           three sliding steps on the wet tile, until her own back slams the fridge door with a
           boom. DAI stays where he kicked from, three strides away from her and from the fridge.

BEAT 4 (0.7s) INSERT

  CAM      close on LIN's hand
  DO       the hand slaps onto the broom handle in its wall clip and grips
  PROP     broom gripped, still in the clip

  BODY     one foot braced on the wall, pull through legs and back, then the arms extend

  SHAPE    she rises from a crouch to a long low spear stance, advancing for the first time
  SPACE    she comes up from low to middle height; the handle points straight down the kitchen
  WHY      she braces a foot and pulls with her whole back because the broom is the only thing

  REACT    the clip snaps open with a crack; DAI stops short, just beyond the tip of the handle

BEAT 6 (2.3s) ANCHOR B

  WHY      she lunges off her back foot because she must not step into his reach; he moves only
