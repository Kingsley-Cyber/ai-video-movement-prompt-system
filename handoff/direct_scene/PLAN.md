## Contract and the natural-language correction

The external LLM directs the whole scene using relevant research, taste and accepted context.
Python supplies questions and local menus, validates proposals, captures decisions and compiles.
The owner clarified that the natural-language work improves **reasoning applied to the content
of the prompt**, not just its printed layout. Natural-language action, movement, performance,
camera and causal descriptions must embody the same considered decisions as structured controls.
Do not produce a generic prose summary while reserving meaningful direction for YAML/XML/JSON.

For a wizard fight, the LLM may choose a planted-foot-to-hip-to-shoulder-to-hand cast and a camera
that keeps its release readable. The accepted description carries that pathway and its resulting
action, rather than only naming "a dramatic spell." Capture concise application reasons, and
include WHY or camera motivation where the selected layout needs them. This is visible directing
output, not private chain-of-thought or an instruction for the video model to run our reasoning.

One accepted treatment feeds the existing compiled directing strategy and resolved canonical JSON
score. NL, YAML, XML, JSON and requested hybrids remain authoring and provider-projection choices.
Authoring form, semantic authority and emitted carrier are separate. Neither prose nor a structured
wrapper may invent a replacement scene. Preserve user locks and report unsupported requests.

Determinism begins with captured interpretation, choices, source/configuration versions and build
settings. Fresh LLM judgment can vary. Accepted-state replay must retain artifact identity.

## Shared decision loop

Relevant need → local definitions/application evidence → external LLM choice, scene parameters,
visible description and concise reason → deterministic checks → accepted state → dependent needs
and projection. Accepted revisions reopen affected coverage and review through the current owner.

A need names an existing pass/sublayer, action/shot/scene target, relevant knowledge and resolution.
Derive it from the current accepted state and captured policy. Relevant required needs must resolve;
optional non-applicability needs a reason. Missing research is not automatically non-applicability.

code does not become valid through that label. Explicit user facts retain their spans; only actual
protected choices are locks. Never-ask delegates artistry, not necessary exact assets or credentials.

## Slice C: accepted treatment, state and strategy

**Depends on A and B. Owners:** current decision validator, strategy constructor, score resolver,
request/strategy/manifest schemas and public finish. **Result:** the sole score and strategy bind
accepted choices and their source trace without replacing existing policy execution.

**Owner-selected first subset: props and hands.** This bounded C/D behavior can precede broader
A/B work: declared entity identities, initial prop facts and authored action preconditions/effects
already fit the existing path. It needs no new corpus or technique library. Replay `prop_state`
from entities and `needs`/`changes` from ordered actions. Reject retired-whole reuse, occupied-hand
use, missing/new-piece identity conflicts, missing locations and ambiguous ordering. Carry the
resulting piece locations forward into each later NL beat. These are checks on declared state,
not inference from free-text choreography or proof of rendered continuity. Existing undeclared
scenes remain compatible. Cross-clip `start_from`, camera-visible state selection, support/airborne,
range, power, damage and genre rules remain separate work; this subset does not implement them.

The packet asks for the following authored records (states are scene facts, not a physics taxonomy):

```yaml
# Entity declaration. Unheld objects use held_by: null and hands: [].
prop_state: {state: whole, location: "Jun's hands", held_by: jun, hands: [left, right]}
# Action declaration. A needs item with only hands requests free hands.
needs: [{object: rail, state: whole, hands: [left, right]}]
changes:
  - object: rail
    state: broken
    pieces:
      - {object: rail_long, state: broken_piece, location: "stuck in the door", held_by: null, hands: []}
      - {object: rail_stub, state: broken_piece, location: "Jun's right hand", held_by: jun, hands: [right]}
```

Declare `rail_long` and `rail_stub` as distinct entities before the action creates their states.
Each change patches one live object. Release/transfer explicitly supplies the resulting holder,
hands and location. The old whole is retired by the break. Missing later prose does not erase a
state: the compiler carries it until an explicit change, even when its location is off screen.

Derive a validated directing trace from sealed captures, active decisions, need resolutions and
research views. Bind its identities into the existing strategy/score path; reject stale/tampered
captures. Preserve neutral-context/mapping admission and ordinary callers. The serializer cannot
turn a pass-only research citation into a newly admitted canonical mapping.

Represent consequential cast/world baselines, action intent, support/initiation/body travel,
contact/cause/reaction/recovery, prop ownership/location/fragments and shot purpose in existing
scene objects. Extend fields/merge policies only where accepted meaning cannot fit. Derive declared
state transitions without inventing omitted movement. New pieces have distinct identities; a
historical whole-prop reference is not an assertion that the broken object became whole again.

Preserve earlier-event magnitude anchors and their checks. Add typed actor/body/world-relative
spatial relationships separately; being below a chin does not require an earlier beat or more/less
speed. Resolve numeric scheduling from accepted timing: cumulative lengths suit a serial card,
while concurrent phases/events and cuts retain distinct relationships. Do not infer frame rate.

The author reviews consequential movement/camera decisions and synthesis against exact accepted
state and selected previews. Preserve concise reasons, observations and explicit repairs; no
separate judge or editable review authority. A changed choice invalidates affected review.

**Acceptance:** distinct accepted treatments have distinct bound traces; replay retains identity;
revisions preserve history and invalidate dependencies; spatial and magnitude relations retain
their own meanings; declared causes/ownership/ending agree. Tests prove declared coherence, not
physical simulation or rendered taste. Rollback is the optional trace/field extension.
